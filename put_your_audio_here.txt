Put your own .wav files in this folder.

- step3_real_audio.m reads the file named in its "input_file" setting.
  If that file does not exist, it creates a test song called test_music.wav here.
- Results of step3_real_audio.m are written to the "output" subfolder.
- The GUI (equalizer_gui) can load a file from anywhere with "Load Audio".
